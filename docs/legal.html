Cookies, tracking and local storage

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services that follow you around. The only measurement is the click counter described below. If you switch between the light and dark design, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that it is remembered on your next visit. It is not transmitted.

Click counter

To find out whether a paid version is wanted, I count how often the “Without watermark” button and the email links for the waiting list are clicked. On such a click your browser sends a request to GoatCounter (operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland; servers at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland). It contains the name of the click (for example “pro-kinetics”) and the technical data every browser sends, such as your IP address and browser type. No values or files from the analyses are sent, and nothing is sent when you only open a page. According to its privacy policy, GoatCounter sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not recognise you on later visits. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing whether a paid version is worth building).
